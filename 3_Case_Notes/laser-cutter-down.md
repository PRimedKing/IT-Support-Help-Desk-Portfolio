# Case: Laser cutter faults mid-production run

**Date:** 2025
**Environment:** Windows 10 workstation driving a Trotec laser cutter, small production shop
**Reported by:** colleague (no names)

## Problem
Laser cutter stopped halfway through a paid job. The panel showed a fault and the head would not home.

## What I asked and checked
1. What was on screen exactly? Took a photo of the fault message before touching anything.
2. Was it the file or the machine? Tried homing with no job loaded — still faulted, so it was the machine.
3. When was the lens last cleaned? Could not get a clear answer, which was a clue.
4. Inspected the lens and nozzle: lens was hazy, nozzle had residue buildup.

## Cause
Dirty focusing lens plus a partially clogged nozzle. The machine was protecting itself — cutting with a dirty lens scatters the beam and risks burning the lens.

## Fix
1. Powered the machine down and let the tube cool.
2. Removed and cleaned the lens with proper lens tissue and cleaning solution (no shop rags).
3. Cleared the nozzle with compressed air.
4. Ran a small test cut on scrap, checked edge quality.
5. Restarted the paid job from the beginning with fresh material.

## Result
Test cut was clean, job completed without another fault. Confirmed with the colleague that the finished pieces passed quality check.

## What I'd do differently or document
Lens condition should be a scheduled check, not something we discover mid-job. I added "check lens" to the weekly maintenance list so the next person doesn't lose a paid run.
